NYX: NEON MAZE

Arcade · Maze · Survival

Control Nyx, a small neon creature trapped in a glowing digital maze. Collect every memory fragment before the monsters catch you — and get out alive!

Download Free Learn More

About the Game

Nyx: Neon Maze is a fast-paced arcade survival game — a tribute to classic arcade maze games, reborn with a neon horror-survival twist. Swipe to move Nyx, collect every glowing fragment to clear the stage, and dodge three hunters with unique artificial intelligence.

The maze changes every stage: new layouts, more enemies, tighter corridors and faster challenges. Each run lasts only a few minutes — but you will want to play again and again.

Three Hunters, Three AIs

  • Volt — chases you directly at high speed.
  • Glitch — predicts your path and cuts you off.
  • Echo — speeds up the closer it gets to you.

Glitch punishes straight-line movers; Volt punishes hesitation. Learn each hunting style before you chase the fragments.

Special Powers

  • Gold Orb — makes all monsters flee for 5 seconds.
  • Green Crystal — freezes all monsters for a short time.
  • Speed Shard — gives Nyx a burst to escape tight corners.

50 Mazes, 6 Acts

Each act brings its own neon color palette and tougher mechanics. From Awakening to Overload, the deeper you go, the darker and faster the maze gets. Every level feels new, with different corridors and dead ends to explore.

Features

  • Smooth swipe controls, optimized for touch.
  • Retro arcade aesthetic with vibrant neon visuals.
  • No internet required — play offline anytime.
  • Free, with no pay-to-win mechanics.
  • Compact size — fast download, instant play.
  • Leaderboard to compare your survival record.

Compatibility

Android 7.0 or higher. Portrait orientation. Portuguese and English.
