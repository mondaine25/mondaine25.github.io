COLOR MATCH

Puzzle · Logic · Brain Trainer

The word reads "RED", but the color is blue. Which one do you answer? Color Match turns the Stroop effect into a rapid quiz. Three levels. Your brain will trip.

Get on Google Play Learn More

About the Game

Color Match is a logic quiz built on the Stroop effect — a cognitive psychology classic. The written word names a color, but it's painted in another. The game asks for one of the two. Sounds easy. It isn't.

Three difficulty levels crank the pressure: more colors, less time, more traps. It's a real brain trainer wrapped in a casual game.

Why You'll Come Back

  • Genuine Stroop effect — scientific basis, not artificial friction.
  • Three difficulties — Easy, Medium, Hardcore.
  • Time pressure — you feel your brain catching fire.
  • Reaction stats — track your average response time.
  • Short sessions — 60 seconds per round.

Who It's For

Color Match is for you if you like Lumosity, Peak or Elevate. For a quick brain hit while waiting for coffee. And to prove your brain is faster than your friend's.

Compatibility

Android 7.0 or higher. Portrait orientation. English and Portuguese.

Frequently Asked

FAQ

What's the Stroop effect?
Interference between the written word and the ink color. Your brain reads automatically, which slows down naming the color.
Is Color Match educational?
Yes and no. It's based on a real psychological concept, but the focus is fun and challenge, not formal teaching.
Is Color Match available now?
Yes. It's live on Google Play, free to download.
Will there be leaderboards?
Yes. Local leaderboards by difficulty are confirmed. Global is being evaluated.
Suitable for kids?
Yes, safe at any age. It can actually help train attention.
