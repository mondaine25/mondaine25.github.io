CLOCK COLOR

Puzzle · Connect · Timed

A clock face. Colored dots scattered across the hours. Your mission: connect each same-color pair without crossing lines — before the clockhand makes a full sweep.

Get on Google Play Learn More

About the Game

Clock Color is a connect puzzle inside an analog clock. Each level places colored dots at hour positions. You draw paths to link each same-color pair. Lines can't cross. The clockhand makes its sweep — that's your timer.

It's a link puzzle with a fresh geometric constraint: the circular face forces you to think in arcs and curves, not grids. Result: a completely different feel from traditional connection puzzles.

Why You'll Come Back

  • Circular geometry — breaks grid-puzzle habits.
  • Elegant time pressure — the clockhand as timer is pure style.
  • Hundreds of levels — increasing difficulty, no grind.
  • Zen mode — no timer, just solve.
  • Clean visuals — golden palette, all about the puzzle.

Who It's For

Clock Color is for fans of Flow Free, Connect or Lines. For a puzzle to solve in the bathroom or the bank line. For anyone who appreciates real minimalist design.

Compatibility

Android 7.0 or higher. Portrait orientation. English and Portuguese.

Frequently Asked

FAQ

How does the timer work?
An analog clockhand sweeps once. When it reaches 12, time's up.
Is there a no-time mode?
Yes. Zen mode unlocks untimed puzzles for relaxed play.
How many levels?
Launches with hundreds. Regular updates add more.
Can I undo a path?
Yes. Just tap on the path you want to remove.
Is Clock Color available now?
Yes. It's live on Google Play, free to download.
