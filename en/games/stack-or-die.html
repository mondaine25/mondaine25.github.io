STACK OR DIE

Casual · Arcade · One-Tap

Stack blocks as high as you can. Each tap drops a moving piece — miss the timing and it gets clipped. The higher you go, the harder it gets. How tall can you climb?

Get on Google Play Learn More

About the Game

Stack or Die is a free one-tap block stacking game for Android by Huster Games. The mechanic is crystal clear: a block slides across the top of your tower. Tap at the right moment. Nail it — perfect alignment. Miss — anything overhanging is sliced off, and the next block gets smaller.

That simplicity is the addiction. No energy timers, no paywalls, no five-minute tutorial. You open it, tap, and you're playing. First run lasts 20 seconds. The tenth one steals ten minutes.

Why You'll Come Back

  • One-tap control — works on any Android, one-handed.
  • Escalating difficulty — blocks accelerate and shrink as you climb.
  • Perfect combos — chain perfect stacks and your block size grows back. Real reward for mastery.
  • Minimal neon aesthetic — clean palette, no visual noise, the tower is the show.
  • Offline — play anywhere, no internet required.

Who It's For

Stack or Die is for you when you need a game to fill the gap between things, but don't want to spend an hour in tutorials. Great if you enjoy timing games like Geometry Dash or Helix Jump but want something cleaner. And it's perfect for trading high-score challenges with friends.

Compatibility

Android 7.0 or higher. Lightweight install. Portrait orientation. English and Portuguese.

Frequently Asked

FAQ

Is Stack or Die free?
Yes. 100% free to download and play. No energy, paywalls or forced purchases.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Play with or without internet.
Is there an iPhone version?
Right now Stack or Die is Android only. iOS is under consideration.
How do I push my record higher?
Chain perfect stacks. Combos restore block size and give you breathing room up top.
Who made the game?
Stack or Die is built by Leonardo Mondaine under the Huster Games label in Cascavel, Brazil.
