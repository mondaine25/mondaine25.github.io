A neon grid that speeds up every second. Cues appear. You tap, swipe, hold — right sequence, right timing. Miss one and it's over.

About the Game

Neon Grid is a pure-reflex arcade. A neon grid covers the screen. Cues pop in random cells — tap, swipe, hold for X seconds. You execute. The grid speeds up. Cues stack.

It's the kind of game that measures real reaction. No hidden RPG, no fake progression — just you, the grid, and how long you last.

Why You'll Come Back

  • Continuous acceleration — starts slow, ends insane.
  • Three input types — tap, swipe, hold. Tricky combos.
  • Pulsing soundtrack — synced to the grid.
  • Endless mode — how long can you survive?
  • Local records — beat your own ceiling every day.

Who It's For

Neon Grid is for fans of rhythm games like Beat Saber, Geometry Dash or Stack. For people who love testing reflexes and have that competitive itch. For when you need to burn off energy in 90 seconds.

Compatibility

Android 7.0 or higher. Portrait orientation. English and Portuguese. Headphones recommended.
