WORM RUSH

Arcade · Slither · AI-Multiplayer

A neon arena. Twenty AI-controlled worms. One rule: grow or get eaten. Tap, swipe, trap — become the biggest thing crawling the grid.

Get on Google Play Learn More

About the Game

Worm Rush is a slither-style snake battle in a neon arena, free on Android. You start small. Eat pellets. Grow. Trap others. The bigger you get, the more dangerous — and the more of a target.

Opponents are AI, on purpose: aggressive, unpredictable and always there. No queue, no server downtime. Open the game and you're in the arena.

Why You'll Come Back

  • Aggressive, varied AI — every match feels different.
  • Neon arena — crisp visuals, all about the action.
  • Timing boost — spend length for speed. Real decision every corner.
  • Zero wait — no matchmaking, no login. Match starts in 2 seconds.
  • Dual controls — tap or virtual joystick, your call.

Who It's For

Worm Rush is for slither.io and classic Snake fans who want something offline, login-free and ad-light. Great for short 3-minute sessions you'll keep coming back to.

Compatibility

Android 7.0 or higher. Portrait and landscape. English and Portuguese.

Frequently Asked

FAQ

Is Worm Rush online multiplayer?
No. Worm Rush runs local AI instead of a server. Upside: no lag, no waiting, no internet dependency.
Can I play offline?
Yes.
How does boost work?
Hold your finger down (or hit the boost button). You burn a bit of length for raw speed.
Is there a global leaderboard?
Local high scores for now. Global is being evaluated.
How many worms per arena?
Up to 20 simultaneously, with adjustable difficulty.
