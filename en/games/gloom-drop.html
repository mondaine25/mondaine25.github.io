About the Game

Gloom Drop is a physics and gravity game for Android by Huster Games. You control a ball falling through dark biomes — neon forests, cold caverns, dimensional voids. Portals warp gravity. Platforms appear and vanish. Your job is to fall as far as possible without crashing.

It's gravity dash with an indie soul. No on-screen clutter, no hidden premium currency. You tap, the ball responds, and depth tells the story.

Why You'll Come Back

  • Responsive physics — every tap has weight. The ball obeys.
  • Unique biomes — each with its own palette, soundtrack and gravity rules.
  • Portals that flip everything — you never know if you're falling up or down.
  • Permanent dark mode — built to be played in bed, on the bus, anywhere.
  • Offline play — no internet, no popups.

Who It's For

Gloom Drop is for fans of falling games like Helix Jump or Gravity Stick who want a more authored visual layer. For people who enjoy dark minimalism and precise physics.

Compatibility

Android 7.0 or higher. Portrait orientation. English and Portuguese.

Frequently Asked

FAQ

Does Gloom Drop have ads?
Yes, discreet ads support development. The game is fully playable without spending anything.
How many biomes are there?
Several distinct zones, each with their own mechanics and look. More biomes ship in future updates.
Can I pause mid-fall?
Yes. Tap the pause button in the top corner.
Best strategy?
Anticipate portals. When you see an inverted-gravity portal coming, prep your tap early.
Does it run on older phones?
Yes. Optimized for modest hardware.
